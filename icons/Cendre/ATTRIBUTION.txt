Modifications by penkin (2026): accent green replaced with Cendre ember
(#ea9875), dark green with ash (#5a504c), white highlights with brass
(#fcba81), and grey paper with cream (#e6d5c2). Geometry is preserved.
PNG sizes and HiDPI variants are rendered from source/folders.svg by
scripts/build-icons.py. Derived artwork remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Other icon names inherit from the system Yaru, Adwaita and hicolor themes;
those fallback assets are not redistributed here.
